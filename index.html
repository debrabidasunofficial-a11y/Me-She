💖 Love Story

The Beginning
Chapter 1: The First Glance

I first noticed her a year ago, somewhere in the endless sea of YouTube comments. At first, it was just a glimpse—a comment that caught my eye. I didn’t know then that this small moment would slowly grow into something I couldn’t stop thinking about.

Chapter 2: The Live Comment Connection

A few weeks later, our paths crossed again during a live comment session. I didn’t speak to her directly at first, but there was something about her words, the way she expressed herself, that stayed in my mind. I found myself engaging in the same spaces she did, hoping for even the smallest interaction.

Chapter 3: The Student Group

Eventually, we ended up in the same student group. Here, things started to get interesting. We argued, debated, teased, and talked endlessly in that virtual space. Somehow, every disagreement became a way to connect. I looked forward to her messages, to the little sparks in our chats that made ordinary days feel alive.

Chapter 4: The Secret Identity

After some months, I created another Telegram account. I sent messages and joined the group again, chatting with her—but this time, she couldn’t figure out who I was. I kept my identity a secret, carefully revealing little hints. Eventually, I disclosed many things about myself that shocked her, and then, finally, I revealed who I really was. The moment was nerve-wracking, but the jokes and laughter that followed in the group made it feel like we’d always known each other.

Chapter 5: Taking It to WhatsApp

As time passed, I wanted to take our conversations beyond the group. I asked for her WhatsApp number, and once we started talking there, it felt different—closer, more personal. Every message, every emoji, carried a weight of anticipation.

Chapter 6: The First Call

Then came the day we finally called each other. Nervousness was in the air; my heart raced, and I could sense hers did too. That first call was awkward yet thrilling. But after that, we began talking regularly, often for an hour at a stretch, sharing laughs, stories, and fragments of our lives.

Chapter 7: The Unspoken Promise

All this while, I kept a secret in my heart. I told myself I would wait until I became successful before ever confessing my feelings. Yet, every day, every moment, she was in my thoughts. I never disclosed this plan to anyone—not even her. It was my little silent vow, a quiet hope that one day, the right time would come.
